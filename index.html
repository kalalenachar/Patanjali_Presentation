📉 The Modern Central Bank Dogma

Modern Keynesian and Central Banking models deliberately orchestrate 5% to 6% annual inflation under the pretext that rising prices force citizens to spend rather than hold idle cash, thereby keeping the economic cycle moving.

  • The Lethal Consequence: A household earning ₹25,000 today requires ₹50,000 in a few years just to purchase the same basket of food and shelter.
  • Stealth Tax on Working Masses: Non-leveraged wage earners watch their hard-earned lifetime savings evaporate silently through fiat debasement.
  • Corporate Leverage Subsidy: Ultra-wealthy corporations borrow cheap fiat, acquire physical assets, and repay loans with depreciated currency.

Capacity-Based Surplus Taxation & Sovereign Non-Tax Revenue Architecture

🍎 पक्व-फल-सिद्धान्तः (The Ripe Fruit Maxim)

"पक्वं पक्वमिवारामात् फलं राज्यादवाप्नुयात्।

अर्थशास्त्रम् ५.२.७० (Tax Surplus, Preserve Capital)
  • Pluck Only Ripe Fruits: Just as an orchardist harvests only sweet, ripe fruit without injuring the tree, the state must tax only surplus profits.
  • Never Pluck Raw Fruit: Plucking unripe fruit destroys both the fruit and the tree. Excessive tax on developing capital destroys the nation's productive base.
  • Tax Holidays for New Enterprises (२.१.१८): New land reclamation, artisans, and startups received complete tax exemptions until self-sufficient!

Complete commercial freedom across civilian economic domains:

  • Agriculture, Crafts & Textiles: Driven by private guilds (Shrenis).
  • Interstate & Maritime Trade: Foreign merchants welcomed with customs exemptions.
  • Transit Insurance (आतिवाहिकम्): State compensated merchants for stolen goods.
